**The third choice when live drifted (docs/86).** When something outside SM rewrites the live files, the user can now choose a direction instead of only pulling: **↑ Keep mine — overwrite live** sits beside Pull/Sync in both the drift banner and the review modal, gated to skip archives, with a preflight (`GET /state/overwrite-live/preflight`) naming what disappears and one forced confirm (pull and push are NOT symmetric — a pull discards nothing on disk, a push discards live content). A provably-clean working copy still auto-pulls silently (unrelated to this — that's the stale-chip case docs/87 fixed). **One directional-trio vocabulary (docs/97):** every surface now labels the same three actions identically — ↓ Take live / ↑ Keep mine — overwrite live / ⇄ Pull & apply (merge) — instead of six different historical labels.

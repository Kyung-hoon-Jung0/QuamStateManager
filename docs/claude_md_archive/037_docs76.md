**Δ everywhere (docs/76).** Every before→after surface renders old/new/difference through ONE implementation: `core/value_delta.py` (+ the `value_delta` Jinja filter, `_delta_macros.html`) server-side, `window.ValueDelta` client-side — pinned character-for-character (`tests/test_value_delta.py`). Subtraction is exact decimal; a Δ renders only where it means something (booleans/nulls/pointers/strings/created-deleted subtrees render nothing, never a fabricated 0). Wired into essentially every diff/compare surface in the app (Review tray, sync screen, plot-click popup, Prev-State diff, Compare-selected, value/Column History, autofit ledger, `/changes`, undo toast).

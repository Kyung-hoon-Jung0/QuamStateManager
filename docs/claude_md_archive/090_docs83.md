**Every numeric parameter, as change points (docs/83).** `core/leaf_index.py` indexes only the TRANSITIONS between consecutive snapshots (median 2–4 changed leaves out of ~8,000 on a real chip), inside the chip's existing `index.sqlite`, and ends up smaller than the curated-eleven index beside it. Pointers are followed. Ordering is rebuilt (not incrementally repaired) on out-of-order arrival; a rebuild MERGES rather than replaces, so pruned-snapshot history survives. Surface: `/param-history/changes` (Trends | Changes tab strip), paged by snapshot. Pinned by `tests/test_leaf_index.py` + `tests/test_param_history_changes.py`.

**Ctrl+Z crosses the save boundary; Ctrl+Shift+Z is redo (docs/107).** The three save sites journal the outgoing change log as user-action units (`core/undo_journal.py`, sidecar `instance/working_state/<key>.undo_journal.json`, bound 200, two-phase — appended only after the save succeeds). **Covenant (as amended — docs/117, 120, and 160 above):** a direct live write happens on an explicit Apply press, inside an armed Auto-Sync session, or as the undo/redo of a change that reached the chip through one of those. A journal step stages its inverse into the tray (gid `jrn:<unit-id>`) and, with "Ctrl+Z writes live" ON (the default), pushes it through the same door at once; OFF is the original stage-only walk. `/undo` routes by the top gid alone; all-or-nothing with LIFO rollback + 409. Pinned by `tests/test_undo_journal.py` (OFF mode) + `tests/test_undo_live.py` + `ctrlz_selfcheck.cjs`.

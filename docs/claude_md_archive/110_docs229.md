**A project opens only with a chosen env (docs/229, customer 2026-09-30).** `/qualibrate/open` refuses (activates nothing, selects nothing) unless the form carries `python` (the landing picker's pick, remembered for the project) or the project has a remembered env that still exists — the old silent fall-back to the "suggested" env is gone. `from=landing` forms get a 200 + `HX-Trigger: sm-env-required` (the picker, always on screen ABOVE the cards, shows a required note and re-submits the Open after the pick); every other surface is redirected to `/?landing=1&choose_env=<name>`. State Load (`/load`) stays env-free. Scope/listing tests opt into the `any_project_env_chosen` conftest fixture. Also docs/229: every `operations.<name>` reader in `core/query.py` goes through `_op_dict` (follows `#./` aliases like `readout -> #./readout_square`; an alias used to 500 Chip Status).

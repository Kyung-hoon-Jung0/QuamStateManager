**A commit never shakes the screen (docs/203, 2026-09-23 — customer: "live state에서 parameter 업데이트 할때 가끔 SM화면 전체가 마구 흔들린다").** The pending tray lives in the top bar's WRAPPING left group, so a commit made the bar one or two rows taller (135 px at 1366, 13 at 1920) and the layout — sized `calc(100vh - var(--topbar-height))` from a ResizeObserver that publishes AFTER the bar grew — overflowed for a frame: a window scrollbar appeared, the bar re-wrapped 15 px narrower, the publish landed, the scrollbar went. Two or three painted shifts per commit plus a `ResizeObserver loop` error, width-dependent (hence "sometimes"); Undo/Apply/auto-apply reversed it. Fixed: ① on screen the body is a `100vh` flex column (`body:has(> .app-layout)`), `.app-layout` takes what is left, `#sidebar`/`#main` drop the calc cap — no document overflow can exist, so no scrollbar feedback; ② `.shell-head` (base.html) wraps the bar and every banner slot in ONE block box, because a flex container never collapses its items' margins (stacked banners doubled their gaps — measured 10 px); ③ `window.TopbarHold` lets the bar's left group GROW but never shrink at the same width (`--topbar-hold`, rows packed with `align-content: flex-start`; released and re-taken at the current height on a width change or navigation; the EXACT observed height is held, since a rounded-up one is itself a resize and looped the observer). Measured in real Chrome on a copy of the customer chip: 0 RO errors, no scrollbar flash, and after the first commit an edit/undo cycle moves nothing outside the bar; 12 pages geometry-identical to main. Trade-off stated: the bar keeps its taller height (~135 px blank at 1366×768) until a resize or navigation. Pinned by `tests/test_topbar_twerk.py` + `topbar_hold_selfcheck.cjs`, 12/12 mutations red.

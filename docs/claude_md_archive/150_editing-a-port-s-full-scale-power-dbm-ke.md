Editing a port's `full_scale_power_dbm` keeps pulse powers constant only if the amplitudes rescale (`P = FSP + 20·log10|amp|`). **Contract (D-43/D-44): every FSP edit door commits the FSP at once, and amplitudes are never changed without the user's Yes.** `/field/edit` and `/field/edit-batch` without an `fsp_ack` write the FSP (the single-field door routes it through the batch door so it owns an undo group) and answer `X-SM-Fsp-Changed: {ports:[{dot_path, old}], gid}`; one client hook (`window.FspAfterReplace`, fed from both `fetch` and htmx responses) shows a non-blocking bottom-right notice. **Yes** reads the plan from `POST /field/fsp-compensation` (`mw_fem.fsp_compensation_plan` evaluated from the old FSP to the written one) and writes exactly those amplitudes with `group=<gid>` — one Ctrl+Z undoes FSP + amplitudes. **No**, or letting the notice go, keeps the FSP alone and says how the output power moved. `fsp_ack=comp|solo` keep their meaning for callers that answer up front (MCP bridge, history revert). Replace All includes FSP fields; pointers stay skipped. The conservative identity-confirm banner (chip-name mismatch) keeps its never-automatic doctrine.

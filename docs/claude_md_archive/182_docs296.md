**A renamed qubit keeps its history (docs/296):** the rebuild writes a rename record into `extras.qubit_renames`; a saved state's era is the record ids it carries, and `core/rename_lineage.py` translates ids, paths, values and whole states between eras by the rebuild's own rule (backward: exact or `None`, never another qubit's name). The chip fingerprint names qubits in the base era (a rename is the same chip). The change ledger reads each position in its own era (`hub_eras`): value history, Column History, Chip Status Trends (with a `renamed` mark), Param History Changes, the Calibration log and Versions all follow each physical qubit; the event where a rename came into force is recomputed qubit by qubit. The snapshot history (no ledger) cuts at a rename instead of joining. Datasets surfaces translate an old run's qubit names by its era and refuse a write they cannot translate.

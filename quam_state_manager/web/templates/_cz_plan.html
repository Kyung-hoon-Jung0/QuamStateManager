The gray loss in each column is the newest population-loss scan of that qubit (cf. arXiv:2112.03708v1 p.11, Fig. 6c): the population lost when the qubit is flux-tuned from idle to that frequency for the scan's duration — a dwell loss, not a gate error. For SNZ gates, total duration includes the flat lobes, edges and padding. The operation card shows stored CZ-plan 2J, t_lim and t_p with their field sources; macro duration minus t_p includes those edges and padding. A remeasure reason is hidden when its source predates the recorded 2J source; uncertain ordering is labeled may be stale.
